## Daily workflows
1. **Walk-in quick bill (POS):** operator picks services/products, enters quantities, applies discount if any,
   records payment (cash / UPI / bank / other), prints receipt. Customer optional for small jobs.
2. **Job with later delivery:** operator creates a job (customer + mobile required), describes work, sets
   expected delivery, optionally takes an advance (recorded as a payment on the job's invoice), updates status
   as work progresses, marks delivered and collects balance.
3. **Expenses:** operator/admin records shop expenses (electricity, rent, internet, maintenance, transport,
   misc.) with payment method and optional bill attachment.
4. **Purchases:** paper, toner, ink, stationery bought from suppliers; posting a purchase increases stock;
   supplier payments tracked, partial payments allowed.
5. **Stock control:** consumption, adjustments and wastage recorded with reasons; low stock alerts.
6. **End of day:** cashbook shows receipts by method, expenses, supplier payments, and the net position.
7. **Website enquiries:** public visitors send an enquiry; staff see and respond from the app.

## Operating rules (permanent)
- No invented prices, taxes, transactions, addresses, hours or reviews.
- Customer data minimised: name + mobile; email/address optional. No PINs/OTPs/passwords ever.
- Operator confirms every transaction and every external submission; the system never acts on its own.
- Financial records are soft-deleted/reversed with reasons and audited.
